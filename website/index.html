Industry briefing

Shakti-Chain
V2G marketplace

A vehicle-to-grid marketplace with a McAfee double auction and Layer-2 settlement. Electric-vehicle owners, fleets, and buyers trade energy in 15-minute rounds. Matching stays off-chain. Settlement uses an ERC-20 token on Polygon with a mint-on-delivery, burn-on-redemption lifecycle designed as 1 token = 1 kWh.

Who this conversation is for

DISCOMs

A clearing layer above existing metering, with a feeder hosting-capacity wrapper after each round.

Charge-point operators and OEMs

Software to monetise bidirectional chargers without inventing the auction and token from scratch.

Fleets

Sell parked energy in the evening peak under a rule that does not punish honest bidding.

Aggregators

A licenceable matching and settlement stack. This is not a consumer app.

Status

Working software demonstration. Indian patent application filed. Assignee BITS Pilani. Not open-source. Not MIT. Voltage regulation and transformer thermal limits are out of scope.
